Each cluster comes from SciBERT embeddings of titles and abstracts, reduced with UMAP and grouped with HDBSCAN. Its volume is measured as its share of all papers published that year, so uneven sampling across years doesn't count as growth. Growth is the annual change in that share over the most recent years.
